Shorts uploader

Shorts uploader is a private tool used by its owner to publish short educational video clips to the owner's own YouTube channels.

It uploads videos and sets their publication schedule, reads the channel's own statistics (YouTube Analytics), and keeps a queue of prepared clips in a folder of the owner's Google Drive that the tool itself created.

It is not offered to the public and is used only with the owner's own Google accounts.
