Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Shorts uploader is a private tool used only by its owner with the owner's own Google accounts.

What data the app accesses

  • YouTube: uploads videos to the owner's channel, sets their privacy status and publication time, and reads the channel's own video list and statistics (YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API).
  • Google Drive: only files and folders that the app itself created (scope drive.file), used to store a queue of prepared video clips and a statistics spreadsheet. The app cannot see any other files.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to upload and schedule the owner's videos and to analyse the owner's own channel statistics. Access tokens and statistics are stored only in the owner's private working environment. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
